Our Scheme

How a Sainand Chits works

A fixed group of members pays a fixed monthly instalment into a shared pool. Each month, the pool goes to one member — decided by open bidding (Boli) among those who need it that month.

01

No interest debt

Drawn from your own group's savings, not a lender's money.

02

Priced by need

Funds go each month to whoever needs them most — decided by the market, not luck.

03

Rewarded for waiting

Every bid's discount is shared back to members who don't draw that month.

04

Fully documented

Every bid, draw and payout recorded and shared with the group monthly.

The Boli (bidding), explained

1

Every member pays their fixed instalment into the pool first. Anyone needing that month's payout then bids — the discount they're willing to accept to receive it early instead of waiting their natural turn.

2

The highest bidder wins the pool for that month. Their discount is split among the remaining members as a dividend, lowering their effective monthly cost.

3

Members who wait until later months, when fewer people are bidding, generally receive closer to the full pool amount.
