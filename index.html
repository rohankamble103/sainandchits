How it works

How a Sainand Bhisi works

A fixed group of members pays into a shared pool every month. Each month one member takes the pool, decided by open bidding (Boli) among those who need it.

01

No loan, no interest

The money comes from your own group’s savings, not from a lender.

02

Money when you need it

Need funds this month? Bid for the pool. The member who accepts the lowest amount takes it.

03

Pay less when bids are low

A lower bid means a smaller instalment for every member that month.

04

Everything on record

Every bid, payment and payout is recorded, with a statement every month.

How the monthly amount works

1

Everyone pays the same. Each month, every member of the group pays an equal instalment into the pool.

2

Bidding decides who takes it. Members who need the money that month bid for the Bhisi through open bidding (Boli).

3

A lower bid means you pay less. When the Bhisi is taken at a lower amount, every member’s instalment for that month comes down.

4

Members who take later, when fewer people are bidding, usually receive closer to the full plan amount.
